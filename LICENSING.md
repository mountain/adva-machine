# Licensing under Unknown

The project adopts **Unknown v0.3**: a public-domain dedication with a voluntary
philosophical statement. The complete English text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE)
and its identical versioned copy, [Unknown v0.3](Unknown-LICENSE-v0.3.md).

The dedication and fallback permissions allow use, modification, redistribution
and commercial use without an attribution, source-disclosure or philosophical
acceptance condition. The nine philosophical commitments express the project's
chosen spirit; they are not conditions on anyone else's use of the work.

Its philosophical center is **exploration in an endlessly open universe**.
Finite arithmetic truth supplies shared, checkable points of departure; the
open logic of finite observers leaves room for new questions, languages and
continuations. The v0.2 drafting was checked against Adva's records on
[finite arithmetic truth and finite observers](https://github.com/mountain/adva/blob/bbfaf3a2597752c96277dc283c0eceec4ffcdb6b/docs/research/0173-mirrors-that-never-close-and-a-question-to-a-waking-ai.md),
[arithmetic truth and its declared scope](https://github.com/mountain/adva/blob/bbfaf3a2597752c96277dc283c0eceec4ffcdb6b/docs/research/0123-arithmetic-universality-and-hypothesized-truth.md),
[exploration and goal-relative stopping](https://github.com/mountain/adva/blob/bbfaf3a2597752c96277dc283c0eceec4ffcdb6b/docs/research/0133-explore-open-universe-goal-relative-stopping.md), and
[finite examples with sufficient mathematical bounds](https://github.com/mountain/adva/blob/bbfaf3a2597752c96277dc283c0eceec4ffcdb6b/docs/research/0183-zhang-finite-example-verification.md).

Unknown v0.3 is independently drafted. Its machine-readable local identifier is
`LicenseRef-Unknown-0.3`; this is not a claim that Unknown is an SPDX-listed or
OSI-approved license. Rust packages use `license-file` to ship the actual text;
Python metadata uses the custom identifier and includes the license files.

## Admission policy for future content

[PUBLICATION_BOUNDARY.md](PUBLICATION_BOUNDARY.md) implements Mingli Yuan's
2026-09-16 direction across the three repositories: future intake is limited to
project-original contributions under Unknown, verified CC0, or material with an
established public-domain basis. Identifying an open third-party license does
not by itself satisfy this stricter admission policy. Unknown cannot release
rights held by others. External dependencies retain their own terms and remain
separate; their existing notices and verified dependency chains are preserved.

This is a contribution/publication workflow, not an added condition on recipients'
existing permissions, a retroactive license change, or a certification of all
legacy material. The policy and its record template do not constitute an
implemented automated pre-upload gate.

## Scope and retained material

- The dedication covers first-party code, documentation and original research
  material to the extent their rights holders can release the relevant rights.
- External dependencies, vendored projects, quotations and separately licensed
  reference material retain their own terms. Their authors' rights are not
  reassigned by this adoption.
- Prior public-domain releases remain available on that basis. Unknown does not
  reclaim rights previously given up or cancel permissions already granted.
- Frozen source snapshots, historical license notices and retained evidence keep
  their original bytes. They record the terms and state of earlier work; they
  are not the current project's license declaration.
- Future first-party contributions should be submitted under Unknown v0.3, with
  any third-party material and its terms explicitly identified. A contributor
  can dedicate only rights they hold or are authorized to release.

The Chinese v0.1 document is a historical philosophical draft. Its statement
that it did not change the software license describes that draft, not this
v0.3 adoption. The published v0.2 text is also retained unchanged; its dedication
and permissions remain available. New documentation is maintained in English first.

Repository contribution and evidence practices, including accurate attribution
and preservation of failures, remain project working conventions. They do not
add legal restrictions to the unconditional permissions in Unknown.

## Version 0.3: freedom, arithmetic truth and evidence continuity

Version 0.3 makes downstream adoption of Unknown explicit and welcomes it
without requiring it. Recipients may redistribute the original or modified
work; they can dedicate their own contributions under Unknown or choose other
terms for rights they hold, including for proprietary products. Those choices
do not reclaim rights in the existing public-domain work. Adopting Unknown
neither establishes endorsement by an originator nor supplies permission to
claim that endorsement.

Part II affirms finite arithmetic truth independently of authorship, ownership,
endorsement or agreement. Participants undertake to preserve the statement,
intended meaning, assumptions, proof or construction, checking rules and
versions, results and scope. Changing one of these does not automatically
inherit a previous verification. Checkers and interpretations remain open to
independent correction; an incomplete check does not by itself refute a claim.
These are voluntary collaboration commitments. Part I does not impose a duty
on every recipient to run a checker or adopt this philosophy.

The unchanged v0.2 document records the preceding version. Existing witnesses,
source snapshots and verification receipts retain their original byte and
version bindings. Dependency upgrades use successor locks and new checks;
old receipts are not rewritten to describe a later license or machine.

The following sources informed the distinctions; they are explanatory references,
not incorporated license terms or an assessment of Unknown's enforceability:

- [WIPO Copyright Treaty, Article 2](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/295166):
  copyright concerns expression, not mathematical concepts as such.
- [17 USC 103(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/103): in US law,
  copyright in a derivative work does not extend to the preexisting material.
- [Creative Commons' public-domain FAQ](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/CC0_FAQ):
  waiver effects and non-waivable rights vary across jurisdictions. Unknown
  retains its own independently drafted dedication and fallback permission.

## Meaning and inherited chain

**`free` is the bounded process in which three related readings, anchored in a
common initial calibration, exchange observations and undergo permitted checked
adjustments toward a task-relative balance; it records which obstructions have
actually been discharged, retains history and remaining tension, and admits only
the continuation licensed by its completed acceptance checks.**

The process and its final acceptance predicate are different parts of this same
word. A completed message exchange, a stationary score, or a stopped process
cannot stand for completion of the whole mechanism.

## 1. State and common initial anchor

A process state retains

```text
S = (contract, initial_calibration, three_readings, history,
     observations, evidence, obligations, pressure, residuals, resource_account)
```

The initial calibration fixes the question and scope; orientation and chart
marking; arithmetic units and interpretations; applicable metrics, scales and
boundary conditions; and the residual already present before adjustment. Each
side is linked to this same calibration by a declared correspondence. Three
unrelated initializations do not acquire compatibility by sharing a name.
For heterogeneous interfaces, the shared starting contract can bind the inquiry
while the semantic correspondences remain proposals to test. An initial anchor
does not assume that a common interpretation has already been found.

An anchor is a declared starting condition, not a truth guarantee. Evidence
against it stops or reopens the current task. A successor anchor must cite the
old state, the reason, the new assumptions and the applicability of inherited
evidence. The old anchor, failed judgments and spent account remain recorded.
Changing coordinates transports the marking and obligations; changing the
question creates a new receiving/acceptance boundary.

Three charts of one carrier, three independent computations, three interface
roles, and three repositories are distinct constructions. Each claimed mapping
requires its own evidence. The finite profile below uses three charts in one
Python runtime; it does not establish independent three-computation.

## 2. Pressure, permitted motion and balance

Pressure must specify both the measured variation and the allowed response.
A profile declares its state domain, available directions, energy components
or other discrepancy measures, scales, boundary forcing and treatment of
unresolved observations. A scalar objective requires explicit conversion
weights. Physical units and conservation are additional obligations.

An adjustment must satisfy every protected condition before its improvement
score is considered. It carries a source state, proposed change, evidence,
correspondence and residual. Improving a scalar cannot pay for a broken anchor,
a lost source, an omitted obligation or a missing observation.

Balance is relative to the declared admissible variations. A differential
profile can test constrained first variation; a finite profile can enumerate
admissible neighboring states. These are separately declared predicates.
Zero first variation alone does not imply a minimum or dynamical stability;
local optimality does not imply global optimality. A witness states which was
actually checked. An evolution needs its own stability or convergence argument.

Nonzero energy can coexist with constrained balance. Fixed boundaries can retain
reaction pressure. An observer may also hide energy in a residual. The record
must retain both the visible measurements and the declared residual checks.
Missing measurements remain `Unknown`, never numerical zero.

## 3. State transitions and outcomes

| Transition | Required evidence and effect |
| --- | --- |
| Initialize -> Anchored | Bind contract, common calibration, inputs, obligations, checker and finite budget |
| Anchored/Adjusted -> Observed | Record actual observations at the required interfaces; retain missing-side reasons |
| Observed -> Proposed | Name one change and its intended pressure response within the remaining allowance |
| Proposed -> Checked | Check anchor, correspondence, dependencies, protected conditions and measured response |
| Checked -> Adjusted | Append the approved state change, its evidence, residual and paid cost |
| Observed/Adjusted -> Balanced | Supply the declared balance witness and the complete blocking-obligation check |
| Balanced -> Accepted | Complete the task's remaining acceptance requirements and name exactly the permitted continuation |
| Any stage -> Rejected/Blocked/Unknown | Retain the reached state, reason, partial observations and remaining obligations |
| Any stage -> Reopening proposed | Record a proposed successor contract/anchor; do not execute it using the old allowance |

Under [Transport and communication v2](transport-communication-v2.md),
`transport` carries presentations across an already shared representation
contract; `communicate` seeks and checks a common interpretation across
heterogeneous interfaces. `verify` checks the relevant correspondences and
witnesses. A proposed learning or representation change
must retain its own authorized scope. The above table is a process contract,
not a new mechanism enum or an implemented event scheduler.

One side's receipt does not supply another side's observation or a required
person's acceptance. Replay refers to prior events without duplicating release
effects. Native proof-dependent transitions still require Rust-owned judgments.
The existing documentary exchange profile supplies transport for reviewed
materials; it cannot perform these new balance judgments.

An allocation such as `33/100` per side plus a `1/100` joining reserve is a
declared resource policy, not the balance predicate. Keep exact units and
charge failed interpretation/checking attempts to the account. A failed join
leaves the committed checkpoint unchanged without refunding spent resources.
Its reserve size and side symmetry require evidence for the chosen task.

## 4. Acceptance and scoped release

A concrete acceptance predicate must require:

1. the original contract and anchor, or an explicitly checked successor;
2. the required three-side correspondences and complete observation coverage;
3. the declared balance witness under the allowed variations;
4. evidence discharging every obligation that blocks the requested continuation;
5. retention and classification of all other residuals and inherited failures;
6. actual human acceptance whenever this task requires it;
7. a consistent resource account and the required durable result/evidence record.

Acceptance names the exact continuation and permitted effects. It does not
automatically execute that continuation, retire source material, erase history,
renew fuel, or close every question outside the task. Exhausting fuel is a stop
condition, not an achievement. A consistent account need not spend every unit.

Research 0157's F1/F2/F3 stays the historical proposal for its declared task.
This continuation interprets closure over the required obligation set of a
named action; it does not clear earlier Pascal human-acceptance requirements or
reinterpret their old `NotRun` records. A nonblocking residual remains visible.
An omitted requirement cannot be converted into a nonblocking one by the sender.

## 5. Executable finite profile: `finite-a2-free-v0`

The [calibration](../../experiments/triadic_free/README.md) supplies a small
external arithmetic model, with original synthetic inputs and no real messages,
native identities, human acceptance or external release effects.

Let the global charge be `(p,q)`. Fix `p=2` and `q in {-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4}`. The
three readings inherited from 0037 are

```text
C = (p,q)       S = (-q,p-q)       T = (q-p,-p)
E(p,q) = p*p - p*q + q*q
```

Each chart preserves this quadratic form. The only allowed moves are `q +/- 1`
inside the declared domain, with strict energy descent. The directional pressure
is `2*q-p`; it is computed in every chart along the transported allowed direction.
At the fixed anchor the independent replay table uses

```text
E(2,q) = (q-1)^2 + 3.
```

Thus `q=1` is the unique minimum in this finite domain, with energy 3 and zero
pressure along the allowed direction. The fixed `p` direction has gradient 3:
the boundary still carries a reaction. Coordinate-cycle closure holds at every
state, including the unbalanced `(2,0)`; it cannot establish balance.

Separately, a cube parity feature of amplitude `a` has directional energy
`(4*a*a, 4*a*a, 4*a*a)`. Averaging its third coordinate makes the feature
constant, with zero visible directional energy, while the residual retains all
three original energies. A pinned per-case bound determines whether this
retained residual blocks the narrow model witness. These cube energies and
the period quadratic are separate measurements, with no implicit sum or
conversion. Missing amplitude or a missing chart observation gives `Unknown`.

The producer uses matrix chart evaluation and cube-edge sums. Separate replay
uses explicit coordinate formulas, the complete energy table and the closed
parity formula, checking every history link and permissible move. Both share
Python integers and the host; this is not runtime-independent verification.
The chart relations are supplied in advance. This model does not perform a
search for common meaning across heterogeneous sides, and its single work
account does not implement the three-share/reserve allocation policy.

The successful profile status is `FiniteBalanceWitness`. It is explicitly
weaker than a native `FreeAccepted`, and grants no external action. Other
outcomes include `BlockedByResidual`, `Unknown` and validation refusal. The
run contract pins the cases, limits, historical references and producer/checker
source before the reported invocation. Reports stay outside repositories until
publication review; partial results never count as completed replay.

common calibration; derive the three readings and allowed adjustments from its

observations through their own contracts; then implement the task's acceptance
